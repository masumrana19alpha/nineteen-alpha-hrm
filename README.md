# Nineteen Alpha — Business Management System (BMS)

Integrated web-based **Business Management System (BMS)** for **Nineteen Alpha**, covering operations across Development (Civil Construction, Software Development & Implementation, AI Healthcare, Hardware/Embedded Systems & Cybersecurity), B2B Supply (Defence Sector & Private Sector), Consultancy, and Research & Development (R&D).

## Core Modules
1. **HR & Payroll System:** Employee profile & document vault, auto check-in/check-out attendance, employee/date filtering, holiday calendar, biometric/RFID device toggle, salary structure (Basic 100%, House Rent 50%, Medical 15%, Mobile & Conveyance), and monthly salary receipt view/print.
2. **Project Management:** General & Defence project setup, Defence Directorate/LOA/security clearance/BG-PG/inspection tracking, milestones, team allocation, and handover.
3. **Task Management:** Task creation, priority & deadline tracking, status board/list view, and collaboration logs.
4. **Business Development / CRM:** Defence & private client directory, lead pipeline, tender/RFQ tracking, and quotation-to-order conversion.
5. **Inventory / Asset Management:** Warehouse & site stock control, stock in/out & requisitions, and fixed asset register.
6. **Account & Finance:** Chart of Accounts (CoA), Debit/Credit vouchers, petty cash, General Ledger, Trial Balance, and Balance Sheet.
7. **R & D (Research & Development):** R&D initiatives, prototypes, expenses, and validated outcomes.
